Our USA headquarters serves as the strategic nerve center for North American operations, providing client-facing services and executive leadership.

Key Focus Areas:
• Strategic consulting and digital transformation
• Enterprise client management and business development
• Project governance and quality assurance
• Market analysis and partnership development
• Executive leadership and strategic planning

Service Specialization:
• Digital transformation consulting
• IT strategy and cloud solutions
• Enterprise software implementation

Service Specialization:
